FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a video take?

Videos up to 10 minutes are delivered in 24–72 hours once the script is approved. Series and larger projects are scheduled with you up front.

Do you write the script?

Yes — scripting is included. Send us notes, a website or a document and we’ll turn it into a clear script for you to approve. You can also send your own.

Can I use my own voice-over?

Absolutely. Send a recording and we’ll animate to it, or choose one of our narrators.

How do revisions work?

You review the video and leave notes on the exact frames you want changed. Two revision rounds are included in every package.

What do I receive at the end?

A Full-HD MP4 ready for YouTube, your website or LMS, plus a subtitle file. Vertical cuts for Reels, TikTok and Shorts on request.

Do you work with clients outside my country?

Yes — amStudios is based in the UK and works fully online with clients worldwide. Prices are in US dollars; just ask if you’d like a quote in pounds or euros.
